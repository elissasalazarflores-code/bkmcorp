About us

Built to Execute. Not to Advise.

BKM exists because deploying medical technology in Mexico requires more than a good product. It requires capital, regulatory structure, technical execution, and deep market knowledge, all operating as one.

In healthcare, a good idea without operational infrastructure fails. BKM was built to be that infrastructure. We bring together capital, regulatory access, technical teams, and market knowledge to make complex medical technology projects happen and keep them running.

Our primary objective is to deliver solutions through outstanding service for our clients, strong commitment, and continuous improvement in patient care; enabling business conditions that foster a prosperous ecosystem for all stakeholders.

What we do

BKM is a Mexican firm focused on the planning, structuring, and implementation of projects within the healthcare sector.

With more than 15 years of experience, we work alongside public and private institutions, as well as strategic partners, to develop efficient, secure, and sustainable hospital solutions.

Our approach combines engineering, strategic management, and deep knowledge of the medical sector, enabling us to transform complex projects into real solutions that strengthen healthcare infrastructure.

Mission

To accelerate the deployment of medical technology in Mexico by combining capital access, market entry, and end-to-end operational execution — creating lasting value for healthcare institutions, investors, and technology partners.

Vision

To be Latin America’s leading platform for medical technology project operation: the partner that makes complex healthcare projects not just viable, but successful.

BKM was created to solve a specific problem: healthcare institutions and technology providers were losing projects not because of lack of intent, but because the operational infrastructure to make them succeed didn’t exist.

We built that infrastructure.

Services

One Operator. Every Stage.

From structuring to deployment to long-term operation, BKM covers the full lifecycle of medical technology projects in Mexico, without handoffs, without gaps.

Execution & Project Delivery

  • Turnkey Project Execution

    We take a project from concept to operational reality. That means structuring, procurement, installation, clinical commissioning, and handover – managed as a single end-to-end responsibility. You don’t coordinate multiple vendors. You coordinate BKM.

  • Medical Technology Lifecycle Management

    After deployment, the work continues. We provide scheduled maintenance, corrective service, spare parts access, and performance tracking for the equipment we deploy, so institutions aren’t left managing complex technology alone.

  • Digital Health & AI Solutions

    Design and deployment of clinical software, AI-assisted diagnostic platforms, and integrated health information systems — built for Mexico’s institutional environment and regulatory framework.

Market Access & Commercialization

  • Regulatory Holding & Market Entry

    For international manufacturers entering Mexico, we provide regulatory structure, COFEPRIS compliance management, and a commercialization framework — so products reach the market without the institutional friction of building from scratch.

  • Selective Distribution

    We represent a curated portfolio of medical technology brands where we can guarantee proper deployment, clinical support, and market positioning. We don’t list products. We operate them.

Strategic & Financial Partnerships

  • Brand Integration Model

    Technology manufacturers who want their products correctly deployed in Mexican institutions — not just sold — work with BKM to manage the clinical, operational, and commercial execution layer. You focus on innovation. We make sure it works in the field.

  • Project Financing & Structured Partnerships

    For projects that require capital structure, co-investment models, or long-term financial alliances, BKM builds the frameworks that make deployment feasible — connecting institutional needs with investment models designed for healthcare.
